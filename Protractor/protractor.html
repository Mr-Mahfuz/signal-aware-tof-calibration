Assembly & Printing Guide

1

Print Calibration Page

Click "Print Calibration Page" to download a single-page PDF. Print it at 100% / Actual Size. Do NOT use "Fit to Page".

2

Measure & Correct

Use a ruler or caliper to measure the 100 mm reference line. If it measures e.g. 99.4 mm, enter 99.4 in the calibration field above.

3

Generate PDF

Click "Generate Protractor PDF" to download a 7-page PDF (1 calibration + 6 tile pages). The scale correction is applied automatically.

4

Print All Pages

Open the PDF and print pages 2–7. Use "Actual Size" or "100%" in your print dialog. Never "Fit" or "Shrink".

5

Trim & Align

For each tile, trim one side of the overlap region using scissors or a craft knife. Align the registration crosses (+) on adjacent tiles.

6

Assemble

Tape or glue tiles together, ensuring arcs and tick marks are continuous across seams. Verify with a set square at 90° and 45°.

Print Dialog Settings — What to Do

  • ✓ Select "Actual Size" or set scale to 100%
  • ✓ Set paper to A4, orientation Landscape
  • ✓ Use plain white paper for best contrast
  • ✓ Print in black & white (saves ink, max contrast)
  • ✗ Do NOT select "Fit to Printable Area" or "Shrink Oversized Pages"
  • ✗ Do NOT use "Borderless" mode — your L3210 doesn't support A4 borderless
  • ✗ Do NOT change the page orientation — the PDF is already set to landscape
